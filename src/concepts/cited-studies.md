# The Nine Cited Studies

Pearson's reference list maps the research base for "the science of critical thinking" in the AI era. Annotations below are contextual, not from the article.

| # | Citation | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Remy R. M. J. P., Schmidt, H. G. & Boshuizen H. P. A. *Cognitive Instruction* 20, 27–45 (2002) | Classic work on cognitive-instruction design — training thinking skills deliberately |
| 2 | Bastani, H. et al. *Proc. Natl Acad. Sci. USA* 122, e2422633122 (2025) | Recent PNAS study on AI and human cognition (2025) |
| 3 | Yan, L., Greiff, S., Lodge, J. M. & Gašević, D. *Nature Reviews Psychology* 4, 435–436 (2025) | Yan (the case-study professor) with Greiff/Lodge/Gašević — review/commentary on AI and learning |
| 4 | Chesire, F. et al. *Trials* 25, 733 (2024) | Trial-protocol study on AI-assisted learning effects |
| 5 | Kops, M., Schittenhelm, C. & Wachs, S. *Computers in Human Behavior* 169, 108650 (2025) | AI use and human-behavior/cognitive outcomes |
| 6 | Mendoza, G. A. S., Ballar, K. J., Yap, J. K. & Deinla, I. B. *Media Asia* 51, 470–488 (2024) | Media/social-media and AI effects on cognition in Asian contexts |
| 7 | Axelsson, C. A. W., Nygren, T., Roozenbeek, J. & van der Linden, S. *J. Res. Technol. Educ.* 57, 992–1018 (2023) | AI tools in education — effects on learning and thinking |
| 8 | McGrew, S. *Curr. Opin. Psychol.* 55, 101737 (2024) | Psychology of AI offloading (e.g., "genAI and the psychology of forgetting" line of work) |
| 9 | Eliseev, E. D. & Marsh, E. *J. Appl. Cogn. Psychol.* 37, 711–720 (2023) | Applied cognitive psychology of AI-assisted memory/retrieval |

> Annotations are the wiki's contextual notes; the article page lists the citations with "Article / PubMed / Google Scholar" links.
