# About

This wiki is a curated, human-readable distillation of:

> Pearson H. (2026). "How to stay smart in the age of AI: the science of critical thinking." *Nature* 657, 873–875 (2026), doi 10.1038/d41586-026-02930-6.

The article is subscription-gated on nature.com; the wiki distills the free lede, the opening paragraph, the nine cited studies, and the related-articles list. The source page under [sources/](sources/nature-2026-critical-thinking-fulltext.md) archives all of that.
